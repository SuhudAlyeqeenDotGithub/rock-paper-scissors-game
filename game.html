Rock Paper Scissors Game

How to Play

You will play against the computer in a series of rounds.

  • Each round, the you and the computer choose Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
  • Rock beats Scissors.
  • Scissors beats Paper.
  • Paper beats Rock.
  • If both of you choose the same option, the round is a draw.
  • The rounds continue until either the player or computer wins 3 rounds. And the winner of the game is the one who wins 3 rounds first.
  • Whoever goes first in current round will also go second in the next round.

Start the Game

You can start the game by opening your browser's Developer Tools and going to the Console tab.

You can also open Developer Tools by pressing F12. Once you are in the Console, type:

game()

Then press Enter to start the game.
